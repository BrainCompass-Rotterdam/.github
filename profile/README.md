# BrainCompass

**We make invisible potential visible.**

BrainCompass builds technology that helps organisations understand, develop and unlock human potential — from the first conversation with a candidate to executive leadership.

We combine behavioural science, assessment technology and practical organisational psychology to turn complex human data into insights that people can actually use.

## What we build

Our platform supports organisations across the employee journey, including:

- **Recruitment and selection** — understand fit, potential and development needs beyond the CV.
- **Onboarding** — help people and teams build stronger working relationships from the start.
- **Talent and performance** — combine assessment data with context and feedback to support better development conversations.
- **Leadership development** — make behavioural patterns, drivers and growth opportunities visible and actionable.
- **Integrations** — connect BrainCompass with ATS, HR and partner platforms through APIs and embedded assessment flows.

A core part of our approach is measuring the drivers behind behaviour — including social, personal and professional drivers — rather than reducing people to a single score or personality label.

## How we build

BrainCompass is a small product and engineering team. We optimise for software that is:

- **Pragmatic** — solve the real problem without unnecessary complexity.
- **Maintainable** — understandable systems beat clever systems.
- **Secure by design** — security, privacy and authorization are product requirements.
- **Traceable** — important changes should be reviewable and explainable.
- **Human-centred** — technology should improve decisions about people, not replace human judgement.

We ship frequently and prefer incremental, well-tested improvements over large speculative rewrites.

## Security & privacy

BrainCompass processes sensitive assessment and organisational data, so information security and privacy are fundamental to how we build and operate.

Our security programme includes:

- structured access control and MFA;
- secure software development and review practices;
- controlled production releases;
- backup and recovery processes;
- supplier and integration governance;
- incident and vulnerability management;
- a formal Information Security Management System (ISMS).

BrainCompass is working toward **ISO/IEC 27001 certification**.
